Automated Customer Onboarding System

A Make.com automation connecting HubSpot, Asana, Slack, and email to coordinate a consistent customer onboarding journey.

01 / CONTEXT

The challenge
to solve.

Customer onboarding across multiple tools depended on manual coordination: creating tasks, updating the team in Slack, and sending welcome emails. This led to delays, administrative overhead, and an inconsistent client experience.

02 / AUTOMATION LOGIC

How the flow
works.

The steps are sequenced to move information forward deliberately.

  1. 01

    Start from the HubSpot form

    A form submission in HubSpot triggers the Make.com automation.

  2. 02

    Create the onboarding project

    Asana creates a project and tasks from dynamic onboarding templates.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the team

    Slack sends internal team alerts.

  4. 04

    Welcome the customer

    A personalized, professionally formatted HTML email is sent to the customer.

  5. 05

    Synchronize customer status

    The workflow updates the customer status in HubSpot.

03 / BUILD NOTES

Details that
matter in production.

01

Conditional routing

Customer types can follow the appropriate onboarding path.

02

Consistent structure

Asana onboarding templates are duplicated so each customer receives a uniform project structure.

03

Personalized communication

Dynamic field mapping supports personalized HTML emails with professional formatting.

04

Resilience

Retry logic addresses temporary failures so they do not silently drop an onboarding process.

05

Connected operations

The flow synchronizes customer status in HubSpot and sends internal Slack alerts.

04 / OUTCOME

What changed
in the process.

  • ✓Reduced onboarding administrative work by roughly 80%.
  • ✓Faster onboarding turnaround.
  • ✓A more consistent onboarding experience across customers.
  • ✓Better internal coordination and team visibility.
