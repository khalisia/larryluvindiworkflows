HubSpot-to-Asana Sales-to-CS Handoff Automation

A Zapier handoff that watches HubSpot deal stages, filters for Closed Won, creates onboarding work in Asana, and notifies the team in Slack.

01 / CONTEXT

The challenge
to solve.

When a deal reached Closed Won, onboarding tasks were still created manually. This could delay onboarding, lead to missed follow-ups and inconsistent internal communication, and create operational bottlenecks just as a new customer's experience began.

02 / AUTOMATION LOGIC

How the flow
works.

The steps are sequenced to move information forward deliberately.

  1. 01

    Watch deal-stage updates

    Zapier monitors HubSpot deal-stage updates.

  2. 02

    Filter for Closed Won

    A filter allows the workflow to proceed only when a deal reaches exactly Closed Won.

  3. 03

    Create onboarding work

    Asana automatically creates the onboarding project and tasks.

  4. 04

    Notify the internal team

    Slack sends the team a real-time notification when the handoff is created.

03 / BUILD NOTES

Details that
matter in production.

01

Specific trigger

The handoff is restricted to deals at the Closed Won stage.

02

Conditional filtering

Zapier prevents deals at other stages from proceeding to onboarding task creation.

03

Automated work setup

Asana creates the onboarding project and tasks.

04

Team visibility

Slack provides a real-time notification to the internal team.

04 / OUTCOME

What changed
in the process.

  • ✓Manual handoff work was eliminated, with no manual administration required at the handoff point.
  • ✓Task creation became instant.
  • ✓Team visibility improved and operational processes became standardized.
  • ✓Onboarding delays were reduced and sales-to-customer-success handoffs became faster.

HANDOFF-READY NOTES

Designed to be
maintained.

01

Trigger specification

States that the deal must reach exactly Closed Won. Broadening this condition could create onboarding tasks for deals that were not won.

02

Troubleshooting SOP

For missing tasks, confirm the deal’s stage is exactly Closed Won, check the Zapier filter, and verify that the Asana template still exists.

03

Dependency map

Documents each workflow step, the data passed between systems, and the credential used to authenticate it, supporting maintenance after an API change or expired token.
